# The Message of the Quran

**Read. Understand. Reflect.**

A comprehensive Quran companion app featuring Muhammad Asad's acclaimed English translation and scholarly interpretation, along with a complete Malayalam translation — designed for deep, meaningful engagement with the Holy Quran.

---

## 📖 Complete Quran Reading Experience

### Surah-by-Surah Reader
- Read all 114 Surahs with beautifully rendered Arabic text
- Side-by-side English translation by Muhammad Asad — one of the most respected English renderings of the Quran
- Complete Malayalam translation for Malayalam-speaking readers
- Detailed scholarly interpretation and commentary for every verse
- Expandable footnotes with in-depth explanations of context, language, and meaning
- Cross-reference navigation — tap any reference to instantly view related verses with their translation and commentary

### Mushaf Mode (Full-Page View)
- Experience the Quran as a physical book — page-by-page layout replicating the traditional Mushaf
- High-quality Quran Complex fonts for authentic Arabic rendering
- Smooth page-turning navigation with page slider
- Quick-access shortcuts to frequently read sections (Ayatul Kursi, Surah Yasin, etc.)
- Sort surahs by revelation order for chronological study
- Download all pages for complete offline Mushaf reading

### Browse & Navigate
- Browse by Surah (all 114 chapters listed with Arabic name, English/Malayalam name, revelation place, and verse count)
- Browse by Juz (all 30 parts with associated surah breakdowns)
- Filter surahs by revelation period — Meccan or Medinan
- Jump directly to any page in the Mushaf

---

## 🎧 Audio Recitation

- **14 renowned Quran reciters** available — choose your preferred voice
- Stream verse-by-verse recitation with crystal-clear audio
- **Background playback** — continue listening while using other apps
- **Lock-screen & notification controls** — play, pause, skip from anywhere
- **Playback speed control** — slow down (0.5×) for learning or speed up (1.5×, 2×) for review
- **Continuous mode** — listen to the entire surah without interruption, automatically advancing to the next surah
- **Single-block mode** — listen to one passage at a time for focused study
- Option to show/hide translation text during audio playback
- Audio synchronized with Mushaf pages — text highlights as recitation plays

---

## 🎨 Tajweed (Color-Coded Recitation Rules)

- Enable Tajweed highlighting to see color-coded recitation rules directly on the Arabic text
- Word-level precision — each word is highlighted according to its Tajweed rule
- Downloadable Tajweed data for offline use
- Download progress tracking with pause and resume support
- Perfect for students learning proper Quran recitation (Tilawah)

## 🔖 Bookmarks & Reading Progress

### Smart Bookmarks
- Bookmark any verse with a single long-press
- Add custom labels to organize your bookmarks (e.g., "For Friday reflection", "Memorization list")
- Bookmark from both Surah reading view and Mushaf page view
- Dedicated Bookmarks tab for quick access to all saved verses
- Each bookmark shows surah name, verse number, and preview text

### Reading Progress Tracker
- **Last-read position** — the app remembers exactly where you stopped; tap to resume instantly

- **Overall Quran completion** — track your journey through the entire Quran with a progress percentage
- Last-read banner on the home screen for one-tap resume
